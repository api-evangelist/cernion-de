Cernion provides a decision platform for municipal utilities, energy supply companies and grid operators. It consolidates market communication, energy data and regulatory information into a synthetic case study, offering read‑only CET/API/MCP orientation and expert review. The platform aims to improve data quality, streamline analysis, and enable actionable insights for city utilities and energy providers.

**Areas:** Energy · Utilities · DataPlatform · DecisionSupport · Germany · Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Cernion: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
